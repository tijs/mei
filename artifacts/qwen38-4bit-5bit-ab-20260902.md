## Bandwidth picture (why 30 t/s is unreachable here)

Qwen3.8-27B is dense (no num_experts), 48/64 linear_attention (GDN) layers
with float32 recurrent state + 16/64 full attention. Every token streams the
full weight set: ~18.5 GB (5-bit) / ~14.7 GB (4-bit) per token through the M1
Max unified memory. Observed `decode.async_eval_submit` 47–68 ms/token vs
`model_forward` 8–19 ms: the step cost is Metal kernel completion of the dense
forward, i.e. weight bandwidth + serial GDN recurrence, not dispatch overhead.
4-bit at 15.66 t/s (63.9 ms/step) is ~89% of a ~19–20 t/s pure-stream floor at
~350–400 GB/s; the reference engine (llama.cpp 10470, Q5_K_M) measures
9.29 t/s on the same machine — 1.67x below the MLX 4-bit row.
